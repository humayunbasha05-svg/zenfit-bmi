ZenFit BMI Calculator

ZenFit™ — Your Wellness, Simplified.

A modern, advanced BMI (Body Mass Index) calculator with age and gender awareness, ideal weight range estimation, and a clean responsive UI.

🌟 Features

BMI Calculation — Instant BMI score with color-coded category (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese)
Gender-Aware Ideal Weight — Uses the Devine formula adjusted for male and female body types
Age Consideration — Special guidance for children/teens (under 18) and older adults (65+)
Visual Gauge — Animated arc gauge showing exactly where your BMI lands
Metric & Imperial Support — Seamlessly switch between kg/cm and lb/ft/in
Weight Delta — Shows how much weight to gain or lose to reach your ideal weight
Fully Responsive — Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
